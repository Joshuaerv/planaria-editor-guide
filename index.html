Step One
Project Acceptance by Editor
🤖 Receive Your ClickBot Notification Automatic

When a project is assigned to you, ClickBot will send you a direct message (DM) in ClickUp. Look for it in your private messages under your name. This is your signal that a new project is waiting.

Open the DM — it contains the project name and a link to the full project ticket.

ClickBot DM — shows the project task link, deadline reminder, and your SPV's name
📋 Review the Project Brief Required

Click through to the project ticket and carefully read the Main Brief field. This contains the client's requirements, video goals, style direction, and key instructions from Julius.

The project ticket in ClickUp — scroll to the Fields section to find the Main Brief
ℹ️
What to look for in the brief Video type  ·  Style / tone references  ·  Key messages  ·  Music preferences  ·  Output format  ·  Any special client notes
🗂️ Verify All Files & Assets Are Complete Required

Before starting any editing work, check that all required files are present. Look at the Main Asset field and any attachments in the ticket — raw footage, music, brand assets, logos, and reference videos should all be there.

The Main Asset field — check this contains all required files and links before starting
⚠️
If anything is missing — do NOT start editing yet Immediately notify Julius or the relevant party and wait for the missing files or information. Per SOP, this step must be completed before starting.
✅
Quick acceptance checklist Raw footage  ·  Music / SFX  ·  Brand kit / Logo  ·  Reference videos  ·  Client brief  ·  Output format confirmed
2
Step Two
Editing Process
🔄 Change Project Status to "Editing" Action Required

The moment you begin editing, update the project status in ClickUp. This tells your SPV and Julius the project is actively being worked on.

To Do → Editing Update as soon as you start
1
Open the project ticket in ClickUp
2
Click directly on the "TO DO" text in the status field — do not click the arrow icon or the checkmark icon next to it
3
Select "Editing" from the status dropdown
Click the "TO DO" status text (not the arrow or checkmark) to open the dropdown, then select EDITING
The Status field highlighted in red — click directly on the "TO DO" text to change it
ℹ️
Why this matters Status updates keep the whole team informed in real time — your SPV and Julius can see at a glance exactly where each project is without having to ask.
3
Step Three
Submission to SPV
🔗 Fill in the Project Link & Preview Link Required

After finishing your edit, before changing any status, add two Frame.io links into the ClickUp ticket so your SPV can access and review your work.

FieldWhat to paste here
Project Link The website link to your Frame.io project folder — copy this from your browser's address bar while inside the project folder on Frame.io
Preview Link A special review link you generate inside Frame.io — open your video in Frame.io, click Share → Copy Review Link, then paste it here
The Preview Link field (currently active/selected) — click the edit icon and paste your Frame.io preview link here
The Project link field (currently active/selected) — click the edit icon and paste your Frame.io project folder URL here
🗂️ How to find your Project Link in Frame.io
1
Open Frame.io in your browser and go to your project folder
2
Make sure you are inside the correct project folder (not a specific video file)
3
Copy the URL from your browser's address bar — it starts with next.frame.io/project/...
4
Paste it into the Project link field in the ClickUp ticket
Copy the URL directly from your browser's address bar while inside the Frame.io project folder — it starts with next.frame.io/project/...
🎬 How to generate the Preview Link in Frame.io
1
Open your project in Frame.io and navigate to the video file
2
Click the ··· (three-dot) icon on the video
3
Select "Create Share Link" from the options
4
Copy the generated link and paste it into the Preview Link field in ClickUp
Right-click the video file (or click the ··· icon) → select "Create Share Link" — then copy the link that appears
📤 Change Status to "Send to SPV" Action Required

Once both links are filled in, update the project status. This automatically notifies your SPV that the project is ready for their review.

Editing → Send to SPV Only after filling in both links first
Select "SENT TO SPV" from the status dropdown after filling in both links
🔔
Your SPV is notified automatically The moment you change the status to "Send to SPV", your SPV receives an automatic notification. No need to message them separately.
⚠️
Always fill in the links before changing status Fill in both Project Link and Preview Link first, then change the status. Your SPV needs those links to do their review.
4
Step Four
Revision (If Required)
🔔 Receive the Revision Notification Automatic

If your SPV requests revisions, you'll receive an automatic notification in ClickUp. Your SPV will have already filled in the Revision # and Revision Deadline fields in the ticket.

Open the project ticket to read the feedback. The SPV's review comments are left directly on the video in Frame.io — use the Preview Link in the ticket to see them.

Revision notification from ClickBot — includes the Revision #, the Task Name, Preview Link, and Revision Deadline
💬
Where to find the SPV's feedback Open the Preview Link from the ticket → this takes you to Frame.io where the SPV has left timestamped comments directly on the video.
▶️ Start Revision — Change Status to "Editing" Action Required

When you begin working on the revision, update the status to let the team know the revision is in progress.

Revision → Editing When revision work begins
With status showing REVISION, click it and select "EDITING" to indicate you've started the revision
📤 Submit Revision — Change Status Back to "Send to SPV" Action Required

Once the revision is done, update the Preview Link in the ticket with a new Frame.io link if it has changed, then change the status back to "Send to SPV".

Editing → Send to SPV After revision is completed
After completing the revision, click the status and select "SENT TO SPV" to send back for re-review
🔔
SPV is notified again automatically Your SPV receives another automatic notification that the revised version is ready — no need to message them.
🔁
Multiple revision rounds? If more revisions are requested, simply repeat this step each time — change to "Editing" when you start, change back to "Send to SPV" when done.
5
Step Five
Download Link Request
📩 Julius Requests the Download Link Triggered by Julius

This step happens after the client has approved the final video. Julius opens the project ticket and selects the "Request Download Link" option, which sends you a notification to provide the final file link.

Wait for this notification — do not fill in the download link until Julius requests it.

ClickBot notifies you to fill in the download link once the project is approved by the client
The Download Link field in the ClickUp ticket — paste your final Frame.io download link here
⬇️ How to generate the Download Link in Frame.io
1
Open the approved final video in Frame.io
2
Click the ··· (three-dot) icon on the video
3
Select "Create Share Link" from the options
4
In the share settings panel, toggle on "Allow downloads"
5
Click "Copy Link" and paste it into the Download Link field in ClickUp
In the share link settings, toggle on "Allow downloads" — then click "Copy Link" and paste it into ClickUp
🔔
Julius is notified automatically The moment you fill in the Download Link, Julius receives an automatic notification or DM with the link. Your part in this project is now complete.
⚠️
Test the link before pasting Open the download link yourself first to confirm it is publicly accessible and not broken. A broken link delays the client handoff.
